## The thang

Line up on the cub, cones set about halfway to the far island

4 sets each with an exercise, a movement out, 5 burpees at the cones, then run back. Loop 8 minutes, rest one 1minute before the next set

- 15 OHP, broad jump out, 5 burpees, run back
- 15 Curls, lunge out, 5 burpees, run back
- 15 Bent Over Rows, lateral walking squat out, 5 burpees, run back
- 5 blockees, bear crawl out, 5 burpees, run back
