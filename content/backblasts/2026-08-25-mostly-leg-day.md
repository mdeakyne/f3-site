The Thang
Laddered stations

6 stations, six exercises. At the first station, do the first exercise. At the second station do the first two exercises, at the third the first 3, etc. until doing all 6 at the 6th station. Going back down drop the lowest exercise at each station (do the last 5 exercises at the 5th station, the last 4 at the 4th, etc. until doing just the last exercise back at the first station). After each station do a movement exercise to the outer cones and back before moving on to the next.

10 Burpees --> Bear Crawl
15 Squats --> Lateral Walking Squat
20 Apolo Ohnos --> Heavy Lunge
25 Deadlifts --> Murder Bunny
30 Mountain Climbers --> Broad Jump
