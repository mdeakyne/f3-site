## The Thang

An F3 choose your adventure quest 

The Tale of the Stolen Gold
The innkeeper slammed down a mug and told the party what everyone already feared: the dragon had raided the village and carried off every coin the town had saved. The Pax lined up, and Farmers took the lead.

*The Dark Woods*. 
Wolves paced the party from the treeline all the way down the forest trail, so they side-shuffled with their eyes on the trees. In the Dark Woods the wolves closed the circle. The party shoved the leader off their chests with 18 merkins and braced low for the next lunge with 12 squats. Then they scrambled up the trunks with 22 mountain climbers, and when the pack came again, 22 more. Even the wolves seemed worn out.

*The Bandit Ambush*. 
Big Toe called the way through the fallen logs. The party leapt them with broad jumps and landed in a narrow pass with archers on both ridges. The Pax drew their bows with 18 rows, dodged the arrows with 7 burpees, and played dead with 18 flutter kicks. When the bandits came back for a second volley, everyone dove for the dirt for 8 more burpees.

*The Dragon's Cliffs.* 
Van Gogh led the party across the scorched ash flats, heels kicking so the burning ground barely touched their feet. At the foot of the cliffs they gripped the rock with 16 merkins and climbed with 20 lunges. Then they heaved the boulder out of their way with 18 overhead presses, and when it rolled back, 18 more.

*The Bone Hall*. 
Wreck It sent the party down a slope of loose bones, crab-walking backward and feet first. In the Bone Hall they lifted the skulls with 14 deadlifts, kicked the bones aside with 22 flutter kicks, and stacked what was left with 12 tricep extensions. Then they crawled through the collapsed catacombs to the dragon's lair.

*The Dragon's Lair.* 
Dizzy made the last call: fight. The dragon opened one eye and breathed fire, and the party ducked it with 7 burpees. They answered with claws (12 merkins) and a tail whip (14 squats). Then came the Killing Blow, 60 seconds of everything they had, and the dragon fell at 9 burpees.

*The Long Walk Home*. 
Nobody left a coin behind. The Pax hoisted the gold overhead, arms locked, and rifle-carried it all the way back to the tavern. The innkeeper counted it twice and filled the first mug before they set it down.
There was no time for Mary, so the toast was the only ending.
